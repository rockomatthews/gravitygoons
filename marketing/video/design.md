# Gravity Goons campaign video design

## Mood

Underground action-sports broadcast from a neon sci-fi dive bar. Competitive,
mischievous, physical, and legible at social-feed speed. It should feel like a
live matchup package, not a crypto slideshow.

## Palette

- Void black: `#050609`
- Panel black: `#0c1015`
- Raised panel: `#131922`
- Paper: `#edf8f6`
- Gravity teal: `#18f1dc`
- Deep teal: `#087f79`
- Impact coral: `#ff4b35`
- Arena violet: `#9a72ff`
- Acid result: `#caff38`
- Muted steel: `#89959f`
- Structural line: `#27313b`

Only these hex values may be introduced in compositions. Transparency may be
applied to them with RGBA values.

## Typography

- Statements and scores: `Arial Black`, 900 weight, tight tracking
- Metadata, token IDs, timers, and labels: `SFMono-Regular`, 400 or 700 weight
- Use extreme size and weight contrast. Do not introduce a third font.
- Use tabular numbers for all IDs, scores, prices, and countdowns.

## Geometry

- Hard rectangular broadcast panels
- Two- to four-pixel structural rules
- Circular orbit/radar elements only as background depth
- No generic rounded SaaS cards
- No pill-shaped buttons

## Depth

Layered: hard foreground type, midground athlete images or gameplay captures,
background grids, orbit lines, ghost type, grain, and localized glows.

## Motion

- Sports-broadcast snap with moments of cinematic hold
- Primary transition: velocity-matched whip pan
- Accent transition: zoom-through for gameplay and final CTA
- Letter/results may slam, stamp, or drop with weight
- All motion deterministic and timeline-seekable

## Required phrases

- `PICK YOUR GOON.`
- `CALL THE TRICK.`
- `TAKE THE LETTERS.`
- `1,000 COMPETITORS · SIX SPORTS · ONE CONNECTED LEAGUE`
- `LIVE ON BASE`

## Do

- Use only accepted NFT artwork, screenshots, site assets, and approved movies
- Show equipment clearly and without cropping away sport-critical anatomy
- Lead with gameplay or athlete identity
- Make LAND and FALL visually unmistakable
- Keep every CTA truthful to the production site

## Do not

- Regenerate, retouch, or overwrite any NFT
- Use real sponsor branding
- Mention investment return, floor price, spectator wagering, or Pink Slips
- Describe unfinished move/payment flows as live
- Start with a logo animation
- Use flat black frames, generic gradients, or empty centered text
