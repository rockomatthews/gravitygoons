# Microcreator pilot scorecard

## Pre-contact gate

Score each candidate 0–2 per item. Require 12/16 before outreach.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience relevance | Mostly unrelated | Adjacent | Direct sport/Base/NFT gaming fit |
| Real discussion | Almost no replies | Mixed | Consistent substantive replies |
| Product literacy | Generic promotion | Some demos | Explains products accurately |
| Sponsor discipline | Undisclosed or excessive | Inconsistent | Clear, selective disclosures |
| Video ability | No usable short video | Serviceable | Strong 10–30 second proof clips |
| Brand safety | Material concerns | Minor concerns | No material concerns found |
| Audience size | Outside pilot range | Near range | Approximately 2K–25K |
| Prior NFT behavior | Indiscriminate shilling | Mixed | Selective or utility-focused |

## Pilot terms

- Fixed fee: $50–$60
- Maximum initial commitment: $75
- One authentic test or demonstration
- One tracked campaign link
- Clear paid-partnership disclosure
- Editorial independence
- No creator-reserved Goon transfer
- No promised investment result

## Renewal gate

Renew only if at least one occurs:

- 25 qualified site visits
- 5 wallet-connect starts
- 1 attributable mint
- 10 substantive product replies
- A follow-up partnership or earned-media introduction

Do not renew after two posts with no qualified visits.
