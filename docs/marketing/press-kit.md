# Gravity Goons press and creator fact sheet

## One sentence

Gravity Goons is a playable action-sports NFT league on Base where owners choose
an exact athlete, challenge a same-discipline rival, call tricks, take letters,
and build an authoritative competitive record.

## Short description

Gravity Goons turns a 1,000-character NFT collection into a live competitive
league. Every Goon belongs to skateboarding, snowboarding, surfing, BMX,
motocross, or skiing and has immutable art, traits, stats, rarity, play style,
and a signature trick. Owners choose the exact token they mint, create a public
profile, challenge a compatible rival, and play a live SKATE-style match with
60-second setter turns and automatic replication after a landed trick.

## The product in four steps

1. Browse all 1,000 Goons by sport, rarity, traits, play style, and availability.
2. Select and mint the exact available athlete you want on Base.
3. Create a profile and challenge an owned Goon from the same discipline.
4. Call tricks live, take discipline-specific letters, and update the athlete's record.
