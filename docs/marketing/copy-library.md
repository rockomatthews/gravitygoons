## Reply patterns

These are structures, not paste-ready templates. Rewrite every reply for its
conversation.

- **Utility discussion:** connect the author's claim to owned athletes, live competition, or exact-ID selection, then show one short proof clip.
- **Base discussion:** explain why low-cost Base transactions make exact athlete ownership and live records practical.
- **Action-sports discussion:** explain the setter/replication/letters loop without leading with crypto language.
- **Game-design discussion:** discuss 60-second decisions, automatic response attempts, Grit, cooldown rules, rankings, or timeout settlement.
- **Collector discussion:** highlight visual identity, sport, rarity, stats, and signature trick without promising appreciation.
